AN OLD ENTRY WAS FOUND.

When the door finally opened, Daniel stepped into a room that looked exactly as it had the night before.

On the desk sat a small envelope. There was no name on it, only a single black mark drawn across the front.

Reaching for it, he noticed something strange. The clock above the desk had stopped, although its second hand was still pointing forward.

Darkness filled the hallway behind him. He turned around, but the doorway was no longer there.

Somewhere inside the room, a piece of paper slowly slid across the floor and stopped beside his feet.

THE BEGINNING OF EACH LINE IS NOT AN ACCIDENT.
